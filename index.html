    A free AI shorts maker that never uploads your video

    OMNICLIP turns long videos and podcasts into vertical shorts with captions. It transcribes the speech, finds the strongest moments, follows the speaker into a 9:16 frame and burns in styled captions. Everything happens in your browser, on your own computer. There is no upload queue, no monthly plan and no watermark on your clips.

    How it works

    1. Drop a long video or podcast. MP4, MOV, WebM, MP3 or WAV. The file stays on your computer.
    2. OMNICLIP finds the moments. It transcribes the audio on your graphics card and rates each moment for a strong hook, an idea that stands alone and a payoff.
    3. Polish and export. Adjust timing, captions and framing in the editor, then download 1080p MP4 shorts, one by one or as a zip.

    Everything you need to clip long videos

    AI clip finder

    On-device AI picks and titles the best moments of a long video, or use Instant mode with nothing extra to download.

    Automatic captions

    Word-timed captions with presets, fonts, colours and positions, burned into the video. Download subtitles as SRT too.

    Speaker follow

    Face tracking keeps the person talking in a vertical frame. Or choose fixed, blurred-background or fitted framing.

    A real editor

    Timeline with waveform, split, trim, reorder, cut long pauses automatically, change speed, undo and redo.

    Every shape

    9:16 for Shorts, TikTok and Reels, plus 4:5, 1:1, 16:9 or the original. Add an image or video overlay and fades.

    Podcasts and audio

    Audio-only episodes become audiograms with a title, level bars and captions, ready to post.

    Questions

    Is OMNICLIP really free?

    Yes. OMNICLIP is free and open source under the MIT licence. There are no plans, credits, ads or watermarks, and you don't need an account.

    Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

    No. Your video is decoded, transcribed, edited and exported inside your browser tab. The only downloads are the open-source AI models, fetched once and then cached by your browser.

    Which browsers and computers does it work on?

    A recent desktop Chrome or Edge with WebGPU. It runs on integrated graphics, and computers with a dedicated graphics card or Apple Silicon are much faster.

    What platforms are the shorts made for?

    OMNICLIP exports vertical 9:16 MP4 files with burned-in captions that you can post to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels. You can also export 4:5, 1:1, 16:9 or the original shape.

    Which languages can it transcribe?

    English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian and Arabic. You can also import your own SRT or VTT subtitles.

    Can I edit a video by hand instead?

    Yes. Choose Edit a video to open the file straight away as one clip. Split, trim, reframe and add captions later, with no transcription needed.

    How long does an export take?

    Exports run in real time, so a 30-second short takes about 30 seconds, and the tab must stay visible. A background-safe export (beta) renders frame by frame instead.
