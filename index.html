            About OMNICLIP

            OMNICLIP by BANKASI MEDIA is free and open source. No ads, no accounts, no watermark. Your videos are processed in your browser and are never uploaded.

            Keep it free

            Hosting and new features cost time and money. Donations keep OMNICLIP free, ad-free and private for everyone.

            Other ways to help: share it with a creator who needs it, star it on GitHub, or report a video that doesn’t work.
