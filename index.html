How it works

  1. 01

    Transcribe

    Speech becomes timed text on your graphics card. Fix any misheard word and the captions follow.

  2. 02

    Find the moments

    Strong opening lines that make sense on their own are scored and titled. Pick your own lines too.

  3. 03

    Export shorts

    Vertical 9:16 MP4s that follow the speaker, with word-by-word captions and an optional title card.
